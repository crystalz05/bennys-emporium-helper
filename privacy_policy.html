Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

Welcome to Benny's Emporium ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled safely and responsibly. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of data we collect include:

  • Personal Identity & Contact Info: Your full name, email address, phone number, and shipping/delivery addresses.
  • Account Credentials: Email and encrypted password, or profile information provided via Google Sign-In (name, email, profile picture).
  • Order & Transaction Details: Products purchased, cart items, wishlist preferences, order history, and return/refund requests.
  • Device & Usage Information: Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) for delivery updates, IP address, and app performance logs.
  • Camera & Media Permissions: Camera and gallery access, strictly when you choose to capture or select a profile picture/avatar.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Processing and fulfilling your shopping orders and deliveries.
  • Managing your account and providing customer support.
  • Sending transactional updates, order status notifications, and security alerts.
  • Processing payments securely through authorized payment gateways.
  • Improving our mobile application performance and user experience.

3. Payment Security

All online payments are securely processed by third-party payment service providers (e.g., Paystack). Benny's Emporium does not store or process your credit/debit card numbers or bank account PINs on our servers. Transactions are handled via encrypted webviews directly with PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party services to operate our app efficiently. These services may collect information sent by your device as part of their API integrations:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: For user authentication, push notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and app stability monitoring.
  • Google Sign-In: For seamless third-party login.
  • Paystack Payment Gateway: For secure checkout processing.

5. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures (including SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit) to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Data Control & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. In compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies, you can request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

To request account or data deletion:

  • Use the Delete Account option directly within the app under Profile / Account Settings.
  • Or send an email request to our support team at mikebuilds.8@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

Upon verification, your account and personal records will be permanently deleted from our primary databases within 30 days, except where retention is required by law for legal/accounting audit purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and publishing the revised policy on this page.
